Transition guide: India's new criminal laws (in force from 1 July 2024)

On 1 July 2024 the Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, 2023 (BNS) replaced the Indian Penal Code, 1860 (IPC); the Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita, 2023 (BNSS) replaced the Code of Criminal Procedure, 1973 (CrPC); and the Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam, 2023 (BSA) replaced the Indian Evidence Act, 1872 (IEA). Offences committed on or after 1 July 2024 are registered and tried under the new laws. Citing an IPC, CrPC or IEA section number for such a case is an error.
